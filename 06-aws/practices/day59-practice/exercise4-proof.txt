=== DEPLOYMENTS AND AUTO-SCALING ===
Rolling Deployment (default):
  minimumHealthyPercent: 100 → zero downtime
  maximumPercent: 200 → 2x tasks during deploy
  Start new before stop old

Blue/Green (CodeDeploy):
  Two target groups: blue (live) + green (new)
  Shift traffic: canary → full
  Rollback: instant (switch traffic back)

Auto-scaling (two types):
Service Auto-Scaling:
  Target tracking: CPU%, Memory%, Requests/task
  Scale out fast (60s cooldown)
  Scale in slow (300s cooldown)
  min: 2, max: 20

Fargate Spot:
  70% cheaper, can be interrupted
  2-min warning before reclaim
  Use: batch jobs, dev environments
  Not for: stateful, latency-sensitive web serving
