=== HOSTED ZONES AND RECORDS ===
Hosted zone = authoritative container of records for a domain.
Public zone answers the internet; private zone only inside VPCs.
Delegation: the 4 NS values must be set at the registrar.
Records: A, AAAA, CNAME, MX, TXT, NS, SOA, ALIAS (Route 53-only).
ALIAS vs CNAME: alias works at the apex, free to AWS targets,
returns IPs directly; CNAME cannot be at the apex.
UPSERT = idempotent create-or-update.
TTL: lower before migrations (60 s), raise after.
