=== MULTI-AZ vs READ REPLICAS ===
Multi-AZ Standby:
  Purpose: High Availability (HA) / failover
  Readable: NO
  Replication: Synchronous (zero lag)
  Failover: Automatic (~60 seconds)
  Count: 1 per instance
  Cost: 2x primary

Read Replica:
  Purpose: Scale read traffic
  Readable: YES (that's the point!)
  Replication: Asynchronous (seconds of lag)
  Failover: Manual promote
  Count: up to 5 (MySQL/PostgreSQL), 15 (Aurora)
  Cross-region: Yes (for DR)

Combined: Multi-AZ + Read Replicas
  Primary (Multi-AZ) → HA + reads + writes
  Replicas → distributed read scaling
