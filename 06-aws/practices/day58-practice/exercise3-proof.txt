=== BACKUPS AND SNAPSHOTS ===
Automated backups:
  Daily full backup + 5-min transaction logs
  Enables: point-in-time restore (to any second!)
  Retention: 1-35 days (configure 7+ for production)
  Storage: free (= DB size)
  Auto-deleted: when retention expires

Manual snapshots:
  You trigger: create-db-snapshot
  Kept: FOREVER until you delete
  Use: before major changes, deployments
  Copy: cross-region for DR
  Share: with other AWS accounts

Point-in-time restore:
  Restore to ANY second within retention window
  Creates NEW instance (doesn't overwrite existing!)
  Scenario: DROP TABLE at 9am → restore to 8:59:59am

Best practice: ALWAYS snapshot before:
  Engine upgrades, schema changes, instance resize
  If anything goes wrong: restore in minutes
