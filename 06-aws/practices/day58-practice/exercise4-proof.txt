=== AURORA AND RDS PROXY ===
Aurora advantages over RDS MySQL/PostgreSQL:
  5x faster MySQL, 3x faster PostgreSQL
  6 copies across 3 AZs (storage layer)
  Auto-scaling storage (10GB → 128TB)
  Failover: < 30 seconds (vs RDS ~60s)
  Up to 15 read replicas (vs 5 for MySQL)
  Zero replica lag (shared storage!)
  Aurora Serverless v2: auto-scale compute

Aurora endpoints:
  Writer: mydb.cluster-xxx.rds.amazonaws.com
  Reader: mydb.cluster-ro-xxx.rds.amazonaws.com
  (Always use cluster endpoints, not instance)

RDS Proxy (connection pooling):
  Problem: Lambda × 1000 = 1000 DB connections (limit exceeded)
  Solution: Proxy pools connections
    1000 Lambda → RDS Proxy → 20 DB connections
  Benefits: IAM auth, faster failover, fewer connections
  Cost: $0.015/vCPU-hour of proxied DB
  Use for: Lambda, ECS, microservices
