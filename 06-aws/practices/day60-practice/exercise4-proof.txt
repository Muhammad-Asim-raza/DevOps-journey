=== AUTO-SCALING ===
Two levels of scaling in EKS:

HPA (Horizontal Pod Autoscaler):
  Scales PODS based on CPU/Memory/custom metrics
  Min: 3, Max: 20
  CPU target: 70%
  Scale out stabilization: 60s
  Scale in stabilization: 300s

Cluster Autoscaler:
  Scales NODES when pods can't schedule
  Detects: unschedulable pods
  Adds: EC2 nodes (5-10 minutes)
  Removes: underutilized nodes

Together:
  Traffic spike → HPA adds pods
  Pods can't fit on nodes → CA adds nodes
  Traffic drops → HPA removes pods
  Nodes empty → CA removes nodes
  Full elastic infrastructure!

Fargate alternative:
  No node management at all
  Pod = micro-VM = no CA needed
  Trade: higher cost, less control
