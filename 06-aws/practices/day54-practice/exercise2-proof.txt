=== IAM POLICY TYPES ===
1. AWS Managed: pre-built by AWS, cannot modify
   Examples: AdministratorAccess, ReadOnlyAccess
   Use: quick tests only

2. Customer Managed: you create, reusable
   Best for: production (specific permissions)

3. Inline: embedded in user/role, not reusable
   Avoid: hard to audit and maintain

4. Resource-based: on the resource itself (S3 policy)
   Enables: cross-account access

5. Service Control Policies: organization-wide limits
   Even admin cannot exceed SCP limits

Policy evaluation:
Default DENY → SCP check → Resource policy → Identity policy
Explicit Deny always wins
