=== AWS FUNDAMENTALS ===
Cloud computing: rent computing resources on-demand
Pay as you go, scale instantly, no upfront hardware

Three service models:
IaaS (EC2, S3): you manage OS and up
PaaS (RDS, ECS): you manage application and data
SaaS: you just use the software

AWS market share: ~32% (largest cloud provider)
Why AWS: most services, most jobs, most documentation
