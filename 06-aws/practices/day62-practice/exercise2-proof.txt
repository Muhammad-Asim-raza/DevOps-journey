=== LAMBDA TRIGGERS ===
Synchronous triggers (caller waits):
  API Gateway, ALB, Cognito, CloudFront

Asynchronous triggers (fire and forget):
  S3 events, EventBridge, SNS
  Lambda retries 2 times on failure
  After 3 failures → Dead Letter Queue (DLQ)

Polling triggers (Lambda reads from):
  SQS, Kinesis, DynamoDB Streams, Kafka (MSK)
  Event Source Mapping: Lambda polls and batches

SQS trigger key settings:
  BatchSize: 10 (process up to 10 messages)
  ReportBatchItemFailures: retry only failed items
  VisibilityTimeout: >= Lambda timeout × 6

EventBridge schedule:
  cron(0 2 * * ? *) = daily at 2am UTC
  rate(5 minutes)   = every 5 minutes

Concurrency:
  Default: 1000 concurrent per account
  Reserved: limit specific function (protect DB)
  Provisioned: keep warm (no cold starts, costs $)
