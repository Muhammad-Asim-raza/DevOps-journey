=== PARALLEL TESTING ===
Strategy 1: pytest-xdist (-n auto)
  Same runner, parallel processes
  -n auto = detect CPU count automatically
  Best for: medium test suites

Strategy 2: Matrix runners
  Different jobs for different test groups
  True parallel (different VMs)
  Best for: large test suites, different envs

Fan-in: collect job waits for all
  needs: [parallel-same-job, parallel-matrix]
  if: always()
