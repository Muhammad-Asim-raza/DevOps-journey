=== BLUE-GREEN + CANARY ===
Blue-Green (26):
BLUE = current production (all traffic)
Deploy new to GREEN (0% traffic)
Test GREEN thoroughly
Switch LB: all traffic → GREEN
Keep BLUE running (instant rollback = switch back)
Retire BLUE after stable period

Canary (27):
Deploy new to 5% of traffic
Monitor error rate and latency
If healthy: 5% → 25% → 50% → 100%
If unhealthy: 0% (auto-rollback)
Only 5% of users affected on failure
Auto-rollback without human intervention
