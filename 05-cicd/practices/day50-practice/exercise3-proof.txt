=== BLUE-GREEN STRATEGY ===
Strategy: Blue-Green
Downtime: NONE
Rollback: INSTANT (change service selector back)
Use when:
  High-risk releases
  Need instant rollback
  Payment/financial systems
Cost: 2x infrastructure (two identical envs)

Implementation:
Two identical Deployments:
  app-blue  (label: color=blue)
  app-green (label: color=green)

One Service switches between them:
  selector: color: blue  → blue traffic
  selector: color: green → green traffic

Switch command:
kubectl patch svc app-service \
    -p '{"spec":{"selector":{"color":"green"}}}'

Rollback:
kubectl patch svc app-service \
    -p '{"spec":{"selector":{"color":"blue"}}}'

Time to rollback: < 1 second
