=== CANARY STRATEGY ===
Strategy: Canary
Downtime: NONE
Rollback: INSTANT (scale canary to 0)
Blast radius: only % of traffic on canary

Traffic split by replica ratio:
19 stable + 1 canary = 5% canary
15 stable + 5 canary = 25% canary
10 stable + 10 canary = 50% canary

Monitor during canary:
Error rate comparison (canary vs stable)
P99 latency comparison
Business metrics (conversion, revenue)

Auto-rollback if:
canary error rate > threshold × stable error rate
canary p99 > maximum acceptable latency

Best tools for advanced canary:
- Argo Rollouts (K8s operator)
- Flagger (automatic canary with metrics)
- Istio (service mesh traffic control)
- AWS CodeDeploy (for ECS/Lambda)
