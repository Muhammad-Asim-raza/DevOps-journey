=== MANUAL vs CI/CD COMPARISON ===
--- Manual deployment problems ---
Manual:
- 2 days planning and coordination
- 45 minutes deployment window
- 10-45 minutes downtime
- High failure rate (40%)
- Stress and burnout

CI/CD:
- 0 minutes planning
- 7 minutes total pipeline
- 0 minutes downtime (rolling updates)
- Low failure rate (<5%)
- No stress, deploy with confidence
